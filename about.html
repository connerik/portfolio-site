About me

My name is Ike Conner, and I am a graduate student
at Michigan State University pursuing a Master's degree
in User Experience. I also have a Bachelor's degree in
Information Science, with a focus on Human-Centered
Technologies. I am an aspiring User Experience
Designer and Web Developer with a strong interest in
creating intuitive, meaningful digital experiences.
Throughout my coursework and project work,
I have developed proficiency in Figma, designing
high-fidelity prototypes for both mobile applications
and websites. I am passionate about the intersection
of technology, design, and human creativity. I believe
great design is fundamentally human, and I strive to
bring that perspective into every project I take on.
